# Pikachu Edition GBC skin for Delta

A Game Boy Color controller skin for the [Delta](https://deltaemulator.com) emulator,
styled after the clear-orange Pikachu edition Game Boy Color. It has the orange see-through
shell, the dark screen bezel with Pikachu on the left and Cyndaquil, Totodile and Chikorita
on the right, a cyan D-pad, black B and blue A buttons, a waving Pikachu, and the speaker grille.

It works on every iPhone, in portrait and landscape. The small ≡ button opens Delta's menu.

## Install

1. On your iPhone, download **`Pikachu Edition GBC.deltaskin`** from this folder.
   On GitHub, open the file and tap **Download**.
2. Open the Files app, find the file, and tap it. It opens in Delta and installs.
   (Or in Delta: **Settings → Controller Skins → Game Boy Color → +** and pick the file.)
3. In Delta, go to **Settings → Controller Skins → Game Boy Color** and choose
   **Pikachu Edition GBC** for portrait and landscape.

## Changing the skin

The Pikachu and starter stickers in `art/` were cut straight out of photos of the real
console by `extract_art.py`:

    python3 delta-skin/extract_art.py WAVE_PHOTO RUN_PHOTO STARTERS_PHOTO

The skin is generated by `build.js`. The artwork is drawn as SVG and the button
positions in `info.json` come from the same layout numbers. To rebuild after editing colors
or layout:

    node delta-skin/build.js

This needs Node with `playwright` and the `zip` command.
